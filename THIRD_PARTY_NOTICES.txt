Third-party notices: LaTeX resume templates
==========================================

Other data and assets
---------------------
O*NET occupation and skills data retain their attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms
in discovery/NOTICE.md. Adzuna's official attribution logo retains its separate
brand/API-use terms and verified source in native/ASSET_NOTICES.md. Neither is
offered as original Stack code under a future project-wide source-code license.
Job matching fixtures now use original synthetic descriptions; see
tests/fixtures/README.md for their provenance.

Scope
-----
These notices cover the copied upstream templates and adapted resume helpers
in the following files, and copies of those portions in exported .tex source:

- agents/templates/jake.tex
- agents/templates/classic.tex
- tests/fixtures/jake-resume.tex
- tests/fixtures/linebreak-skills-resume.tex

Stack preserves its own existing code and separate third-party license terms.
This file records template notices; it does not select a project-wide license.

Provenance
----------
The Jake template and fixtures identify Jake Gutierrez and sb2nov/resume in
their existing headers. Their preamble and custom commands derive from the
Jake template. Classic adapts the same resume helper structure for its own
layout; these upstream notices are also retained there.

Stack introduced these templates in commit
3b58a5bb92a3a4a1f75c3c0b0ab8018814ce54f7 and later adapted the test fixtures.
The original import did not record an exact upstream revision. The immutable
references below were inspected to verify the template lineage and applicable
notices, rather than claiming an undocumented exact import revision.

Jake Gutierrez: archived upstream template and license verified at
https://github.com/jakegut/resume/tree/3e64a933141a7cda3a328f0743f73a201628a4ba

Sourabh Bajaj: upstream base and license verified at the last revision before
the archived Jake version, preserving its original 2015 copyright notice:
https://github.com/sb2nov/resume/tree/1021abf6948b52838390ad008045dd8c60ae0095

Both full notices are included below and directly in the .tex files so source
exports and releases containing the templates carry them without needing a
separate root-level file. Keep the embedded comments when redistributing or
adapting template source. These comments do not appear on rendered resumes.
