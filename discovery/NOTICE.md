# Occupational data

Includes unmodified occupation_data.csv, job_titles.csv, and sample_of_reported_titles.csv from the O*NET 31.0 Database, downloaded September 27, 2026.

# Skills vocabulary

agents/data/skills.json is derived from Technology Skills.txt in the O*NET 30.1 Database, downloaded September 29, 2026, by scripts/build-skills.py. Product names are shortened to common forms (for example "Structured query language SQL" to SQL), generic software categories are removed, and matching rules are added.

Source: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution: O*NET Resource Center, U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration. No endorsement by the Department of Labor is implied.

Employer URLs are discovery candidates, not assertions of live openings. Public page crawling honors robots and does not bypass access controls. GitHub sources are used only to discover destination links.
