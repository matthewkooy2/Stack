# Occupational data

Includes unmodified occupation_data.csv, job_titles.csv, and sample_of_reported_titles.csv from the O*NET 31.0 Database, downloaded September 27, 2026.

Source: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution: O*NET Resource Center, U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration.
Stack adds occupation-family labels and a conservative title matching algorithm. No endorsement by the Department of Labor is implied.

Employer URLs are discovery candidates, not assertions of live openings. Public page crawling honors robots and does not bypass access controls. GitHub sources are used only to discover destination links.
